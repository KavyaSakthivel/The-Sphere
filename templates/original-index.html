01 / A SPACE THAT FEELS LIKE YOURS

Wellness,
beyond the usual.

We believe wellness is more than a workout, a treatment or a moment of escape. It is how you feel when you leave a room. The people you meet. The conversations you have. The rituals you return to.

The Sphere is an intimate women’s wellness club created around meaningful experiences, conscious living and genuine connection.

Here, wellness isn’t another task on your list.
It is time to pause. To breathe. To simply be.

BEYOND EVERY ROLE YOU HOLD

A founder. A mother. A leader.
A daughter. A partner. A friend.
And before all of that, you.

You may be building something extraordinary.
The Sphere exists to create a little more room for the woman behind it all.
A place where you don’t have to perform, achieve or keep up. You simply arrive.
